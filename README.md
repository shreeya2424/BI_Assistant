## BI Assistant

The app aims to design an intelligent BI system that helps small and medium businesses analyze their sales or operational data easily. By allowing users to upload data files (such as CSV or Excel sheets), the system automatically performs data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and prediction — presenting actionable business insights without requiring technical skills. It also incorporates an AI chatbot that users can use to better understand their data.

Checkout the app here - https://biassistant.streamlit.app/
